# Brand — StockProof

_Status: active_

## Position

Evidence infrastructure for tokenized equities on Solana. The voice is direct, technical, and explicit about uncertainty. StockProof never sounds like a trading terminal or investment recommendation.

## Palette

- Ink: `#111c24` — primary infrastructure surface.
- Canvas: `#f1efe8` — warm research-document background.
- Paper: `#fffdf8` — evidence cards.
- Solana verification: `#087c61` — successful onchain checks only.
- Evidence blue: `#1769ff` — actions, provenance, and focus.
- Caution amber: `#9b6400` — incomplete or conflicting evidence.
- Failure coral: `#b2383f` — failed deterministic checks only.

## Typography

- Instrument Sans for interface and display copy.
- IBM Plex Mono for symbols, slots, multipliers, addresses, and evidence IDs.
- Newsreader italic for the editorial hero accent and evidence brief.

## Interaction

Fast 100 ms micro-feedback, visible blue focus rings, real buttons and links, no decorative entrance animation, and a complete reduced-motion override.
