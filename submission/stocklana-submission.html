The gap

Most tokenized-stock interfaces flatten a complex asset into a logo and a quote. A protocol needs harder answers before it displays, routes, or accepts that asset: is this the official mint, is its corporate-action multiplier current, can transfers be paused, and do reported custody shares cover reported circulation?

The multiplier case is concrete. NFLXx currently carries an effective onchain Scaled UI multiplier of 10.0 after a split. An app that reads the raw token amount and displays it is wrong by a factor of ten, and no price feed tells it so. StockProof reads that multiplier from the Token-2022 mint account and reconciles it against issuer metadata.

StockProof issues one deterministic passport by comparing official xStocks metadata with Solana mainnet. It never predicts price direction, recommends a trade, or turns missing evidence into confidence.

Why Solana

Solana is the verification surface, not a logo. StockProof reads the Token-2022 account that controls displayed balances and exposes administrative controls, then binds those facts to issuer evidence at a confirmed slot. Remove Solana and the product cannot answer the two questions that matter: what balance will a user actually see, and can the transfer be stopped.

Coverage, stated precisely

xStocks publishes 928 tokens with a Solana deployment. StockProof pins 20 of them — the large-cap names an integrator is most likely to accept as collateral or route:

AAPLx · NVDAx · MSFTx · TSLAx · AMZNx · GOOGLx · METAx · AVGOx · NFLXx · AMDx · PLTRx · MSTRx · COINx · JPMx · Vx · UBERx · ORCLx · QQQx · SPYx · GLDx

Each mint is pinned in source, not discovered at runtime. That is deliberate: dynamically accepting whatever mint issuer metadata returns would cover all 928 assets and would also destroy the check the product exists to perform. Coverage is a reviewed list, and npm run live:verify is the gate that keeps it honest — it fails if any pinned asset stops reconciling.

Current prototype

  • 20 pinned xStocks with live passports, plus query aliases such as “google”, “netflix”, or “gold”.
  • Six deterministic checks. Identity, token-program, multiplier, reserves, and transfer controls are required: an unreadable pause extension abstains rather than passing. A set freeze authority is disclosed, not hidden.
  • The route refuses a missing, unsupported, ambiguous, or repeated symbol instead of substituting a default asset.
  • Read-only by contract: non-GET methods return 405.
  • Frozen fixture fallback is marked UNVERIFIABLE, and the audit surface labels it a fixture rather than an issued passport.
  • Automated tests include validation of the published JSON Schema against real route output, alongside a twenty-asset live integration gate and a committed rendered-product QA suite.
  • Cross-origin versioned JSON, published schema, and a working five-minute sample consumer policy.

Declared boundary

Proof-of-reserves is issuer-reported. StockProof checks its internal coverage ratio and applies a 72-hour freshness policy; it does not independently audit custody or reconcile reported circulation with onchain supply. The consumer policy runs inside this prototype; no external protocol integration is claimed. Built with React, Vite, and lucide-react, reading public xStocks endpoints and Solana mainnet RPC.
