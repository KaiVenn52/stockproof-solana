The gap

Most tokenized-stock interfaces flatten a complex asset into a logo and a quote. A protocol needs harder answers before it displays, routes, or accepts that asset: is this the official mint, is its corporate-action multiplier current, can transfers be paused, and do reported custody shares cover reported circulation?

StockProof issues one deterministic passport by comparing official xStocks metadata with Solana mainnet. It never predicts price direction, recommends a trade, or turns missing evidence into confidence.

Why Solana

Solana is the verification surface, not a logo. StockProof reads the Token-2022 account that controls displayed balances and exposes administrative controls, then binds those facts to issuer evidence at a confirmed slot.

Current prototype

  • AAPLx, NVDAx, TSLAx, and QQQx live passports.
  • No API key, wallet, or exchange credential required.
  • Expandable checks with source endpoints and timestamps.
  • Frozen fixture fallback is marked UNVERIFIABLE when live evidence fails.
  • Twenty-four automated tests plus a four-asset live integration probe.
  • Cross-origin versioned JSON, published schema, and a working five-minute sample consumer policy.

Declared boundary

Proof-of-reserves is issuer-reported. StockProof checks its internal coverage ratio and applies a 72-hour freshness policy; it does not independently audit custody or reconcile reported circulation with onchain supply. The consumer policy runs inside this prototype; no external protocol integration is claimed. Research infrastructure only. Legal availability varies by jurisdiction.
