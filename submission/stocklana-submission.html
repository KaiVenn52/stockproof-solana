The gap

Most tokenized-stock interfaces flatten a complex asset into a logo and a quote. A protocol needs harder answers before it displays, routes, or accepts that asset: is this the official mint, is its corporate-action multiplier current, can transfers be paused, and do reported custody shares cover reported circulation?

StockProof issues one deterministic passport by reconciling official xStocks data with Solana mainnet. It never predicts price direction, recommends a trade, or converts missing evidence into confidence.

Why Solana

Solana is the verification surface, not a logo. StockProof reads the Token-2022 account that controls displayed balances and exposes live administrative controls, then binds those facts to issuer evidence at a confirmed slot. The resulting passport is designed to become a composable policy input for wallets, swap interfaces, lending markets, index products, and agents.

Current prototype

  • Live AAPLx, NVDAx, TSLAx, and QQQx passports.
  • No API key, wallet, or exchange credential required.
  • Expandable checks with source endpoints and timestamps.
  • Honest fixture fallback when live evidence is unavailable.
  • Eight unit tests plus a four-asset live integration probe.

Declared boundary

Proof-of-reserves is issuer-reported. StockProof verifies coverage, freshness, and agreement with chain state; it does not independently audit the offchain custodian. Research infrastructure only. Legal availability varies by jurisdiction.
